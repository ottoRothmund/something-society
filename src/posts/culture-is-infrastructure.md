title: Culture is infrastructure.
date: 2026-09-03
summary: Five positions the institution is built on.
---

The institutions that lost consensus will not explain what comes next. We are building the record while it is still being written.

Five positions, in order:

1. Culture determines what capital can see.
2. Small coherent groups outrun large indifferent systems.
3. A publication should recruit, not merely report.
4. Software and hardware are beliefs with interfaces.
5. Ownership is the only durable editorial policy.

Each division works alone. Together, they compound attention into agency.
